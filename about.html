Our Story

About JJG Paper Bag

Juang Jia Guoo Co., Ltd. is a professional paper bag manufacturer in Taiwan with over 70 years of production experience, specializing in the development and manufacturing of various food paper bags and commercial paper bags.

We provide greaseproof paper bags, laminated paper bags, kraft paper bags, white kraft paper bags, handle paper bags, shopping bags, and custom-printed paper bags, serving the needs of restaurants, chain brands, food factories, retail channels, and a wide range of corporate clients.

With "quality, safety, and reliable supply" as our core values, our products comply with food safety standards and international certifications, ensuring stable production capacity, large-volume order support, and dependable delivery schedules.

Our Vision

To be the world's most trusted paper bag manufacturer, leading the industry toward 100% sustainable packaging solutions.

Our Mission

Delivering premium quality, food-safe paper packaging through innovation, integrity, and environmental responsibility.

Our Values

Quality, safety, and reliable supply — certified with ISO 22000, HACCP, FSC, and FSSC 22000 international standards.

Milestones

Our History

1960s

Company Founded

Juang Jia Guoo was established in Taichung, Taiwan, beginning our journey in paper bag manufacturing. We started with a commitment to local businesses, providing reliable and quality packaging solutions.

1980s

Export Expansion

Expanded our operations to serve international markets. By establishing robust global export capabilities, we brought Taiwanese manufacturing excellence to the world stage.

2000s

Quality Certifications

Committed to the highest standards, we achieved ISO 22000, HACCP, FSC, and FSSC 22000 certifications, cementing our dedication to food safety and environmental sustainability.

2020s

PFAS-Free Innovation

Pioneered PFAS-free, biodegradable paper bag technology. Today, we proudly serve over 100 countries worldwide with sustainable, cutting-edge packaging solutions.
